      A field guide

      How to bloom.

      Guess the same mystery word as your friends, on your own private board. The host chooses 4, 5, 6, or 7 letters for the room.

      1. You have six guesses and three minutes each round. Submit a real English word with your room’s chosen number of letters.
      2. Green means the letter is in the right spot. Gold means it belongs somewhere else. Gray means it’s not in the word. Repeated letters are marked only as often as they appear.
      3. Every solve earns one point, whenever you find it. Other players can keep guessing after you finish.
      4. The answer blooms when everyone is done or time runs out. The host starts the next round. Most points wins; ties are celebrated.

      Invite friends before starting. Want a quiet moment? Solo rooms work, too.
